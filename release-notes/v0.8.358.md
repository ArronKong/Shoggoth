## English

- Native chat can discover the configured Feishu/Lark CLI connection, verify the account, and read workbook details. Creating or writing spreadsheets and Bitable records requires confirmation of the final action.
- Background-task cards show the full task title. Long titles wrap in the task details and chat layouts.
- Workspace execution reports known scope and approval rejections directly. Commands that may already have started retain their uncertain status, and a later call can recheck a temporary sandbox failure.
- Recovering a completed turn keeps its original usage fields, preventing transcript conflicts when the service restarts after a disk-full error.
- Updated keyboard focus styling across the app.

## 简体中文

- 原生聊天可以发现已配置的飞书 CLI 连接、验证账号和读取工作簿信息。创建或写入电子表格及多维表格时，需要确认最终操作。
- 后台任务卡恢复显示完整标题。长标题在任务详情和聊天布局中可换行显示。
- 工作区执行会给出明确的范围错误或审批拒绝原因。可能已经启动的命令保留未知状态，后续调用可以重新检查临时的沙箱故障。
- 恢复已结束的回合时沿用原有用量字段，避免磁盘写满后重启服务时发生转录冲突。
- 调整了应用各处的键盘焦点样式。
