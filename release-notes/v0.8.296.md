## English

### Highlights

- **Chat and group chat.** Live work appears in the assistant bubble with per-turn timing and a detail view. Interrupted or recovered runs keep their visible activity. Quote and mention controls are more consistent, group messages are included in global search, and initial chat history retries while the background service starts.
- **Memory and nightly review.** Native agents use their own memory by default and can share eligible conversation excerpts across their own sessions. Corrections and forgetting follow consistent rules, and rejected writes explain how to recover. Nightly review supports Grok and Antigravity and retains a completed response even if its worker stops immediately afterward.
- **Native runtimes and models.** Native CLI compatibility checks the capabilities the app needs. Model lists are cached across restarts and warmed in the background. Native agents can use their own default model.
- **Settings, skills, and connectors.** Settings use a unified layout with backend details in dialogs. Skills and connector lists share a scrollable card layout. Several connector icons were refreshed, and the community-maintained 12306 preset was removed from the built-in catalog.
- **Dashboard, Inspiration, and scheduled tasks.** Loading placeholders match the content layout, the Dashboard shows one activity per inspiration note, Inspiration cards and detail views were refined, and scheduled-task cards align with the calendar.

## 简体中文

### 更新内容

- **聊天与群聊。** 助理气泡内展示实时执行活动、每轮耗时和详情。中断或恢复的运行保留可见活动。引用和提及操作更一致，全局搜索包含群聊消息，后台服务启动期间会自动重试读取首屏聊天历史。
- **记忆与夜间复盘。** 原生助理默认使用自己的记忆，同一助理的不同会话可以共享符合条件的对话摘录。纠正和忘记记忆采用一致规则，写入被拒绝时会提示如何恢复。夜间复盘支持 Grok 与 Antigravity，工作进程在回复后立即结束时也能保留已完成的回答。
- **原生运行时与模型。** 原生 CLI 兼容性按应用所需能力检测。模型列表支持跨重启缓存并在后台预热，原生助理可以设置自己的默认模型。
- **设置、技能与连接器。** 设置使用统一布局，并通过弹窗查看后端详情。技能和连接器列表共用可滚动的卡片布局。更新了多款连接器图标，内置目录移除了社区维护的 12306 预设。
- **Dashboard、灵感与定时任务。** 加载占位与内容布局一致，Dashboard 每张灵感便签只展示一条活动，调整了灵感卡片和详情，并对齐了定时任务列表卡片与日历。
